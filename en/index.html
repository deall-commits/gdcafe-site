GD CAFE — a chandelier café in Cheongdam, Seoul

GD CAFE is a café on the 2nd floor of 716 Samseong-ro in Cheongdam-dong, Gangnam-gu, Seoul, in the building with Olive Young Cheongdam Station on the ground floor. It is about 100 m, a 1–2 minute walk from Exit 9 of Cheongdam Station (Line 7), and it is open every day from 09:00 to 20:00 (last order 19:30).

The space

A large crystal chandelier hangs in the middle of the hall, a dark arch frames the white marble-look bar, tall 2nd-floor windows look out over street trees, and a red brick wall meets white panel moulding. GD stands for “A Good Day”.
