GD CAFE – a chandelier cafe in Cheongdam, Seoul

GD CAFE is a cafe in Cheongdam-dong, Gangnam-gu, Seoul, about 100 m from Exit 9 of Cheongdam Station (Line 7). You'll find it on the 2nd floor of the building with the Olive Young Cheongdam Station branch. It's open every day from 09:00 to 20:00, and last orders are at 19:30. GD stands for “A Good Day”.

The Space

The space

A large crystal chandelier hangs from the centre of the main room's ceiling, and a dark arch frames the white marble-effect bar. The seats by the big 2nd-floor windows look out onto street trees, and further inside, a red brick wall meets a white wall with moulding.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where is GD CAFE?

GD CAFE is at 2F, 716 Samseong-ro, Gangnam-gu, Seoul, in Cheongdam-dong. It's about 100 m from Exit 9 of Cheongdam Station (Line 7), in the building with the Olive Young Cheongdam Station branch.

What are GD CAFE's opening hours?

GD CAFE is open every day from 09:00 to 20:00, with no closing days. Last orders are at 19:30.

Is there an English menu?

Yes. This site lists the menu and prices in English, with each item's Korean name shown alongside.

Is there Wi-Fi?

Yes. GD CAFE has Wi-Fi for customers.

Do you have decaf coffee?

Yes. GD CAFE's Americano can be made with decaf beans, and chamomile tea is a caffeine-free option too.

Can I rent the whole cafe or film there?

Yes. GD CAFE offers private hire of its entire 2nd-floor space. For filming, group or event bookings, call +82-10-3431-8482.

Can I make a reservation or order by phone?

Yes. GD CAFE takes phone orders and advance reservations at +82-10-3431-8482.
